That Broke Me

I was losing 4–5 hours a day to the commute - rain or shine, round trip, standing the whole way just to shave off some time - then watching my baby through a CCTV feed just to feel close. Then something clicked: this wasn't a scheduling problem I could fix. It was time for a bigger change.

Rejected.

✕ ✕ 2 rejections. Not giving up.

I applied to Athena. Rejected. Applied again. Rejected again. It stung, but I already knew this was the only agency I wanted to be part of.

Era 2 · Getting In
While I Waited to Try Again

The transition

Failed my first attempt at Athena.

Rather than wait around for the next opening, I used that time to invest in myself.

Took Coursera courses in social media marketing and management, advertising, CRM with HubSpot, scheduling with Hootsuite, data analysis with Power BI, and accounting.

Built the skills I didn't have yet, on my own time.

Applied again. Tried a third time. Eventually, it worked.

Passed.
Officially In.

✕ ✕ ✓ 2 misses, 1 yes

On 6/13/25, that persistence finally paid off and I started as Executive Partner, Athena.
