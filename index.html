Era 2 · Getting In
While I Waited to Try Again

The transition

Failed my first attempt at Athena.

Rather than wait around for the next opening, I used that time to invest in myself.

Took Coursera courses in social media marketing and management, advertising, CRM with HubSpot, scheduling with Hootsuite, and data analysis with Power BI.

Built the skills I didn't have yet, on my own time.

Applied again. Tried a third time. Eventually, it worked.

Passed.
Officially In.

✕ ✕ ✓ 2 misses, 1 yes

On 6/13/25, that persistence finally paid off and I started as Executive Assistant, Athena.
